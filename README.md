# Management Skill

An organizational operations engine built on a 6-axis × 6-scale matrix. Diagnoses, designs, and prescribes management patterns across organizational design, talent pipeline, execution systems, culture engineering, operating finance, and crisis/transition management — all calibrated to company size.

## Why This Exists

Business strategy skills tell you *what* to do. This skill tells you *how to run the organization that does it*. Harvard research shows 90% of organizations fail at strategy execution — not because the strategy was wrong, but because organizational operations weren't built for it.

## Core Architecture

- **6 Axes**: Org Design (M1) · Talent (M2) · Execution Systems (M3) · Culture (M4) · Operating Finance (M5) · Crisis & Transition (M6)
- **6 Scale Stages**: S1 (1–5) · S2 (6–15) · S3 (16–50) · S4 (51–150) · S5 (151–500) · S6 (500+)
- **Cross-cutting principle**: The right answer flips depending on company size. What works at 15 people breaks at 150.
- **3 Modes**: Diagnose · Design · Prescribe

## Key Frameworks

Drucker (MBO) · Mintzberg (5 org types) · McKinsey 7S · Collins (Level 5, Flywheel) · Schein (3-layer culture) · Kotter (8-step change) · Lencioni (5 dysfunctions) · Grove (OKR, High Output Management) · Toyota (TPS) · Dalio (radical transparency) · Harnish (Scaling Up) · Greiner (growth crisis model)
